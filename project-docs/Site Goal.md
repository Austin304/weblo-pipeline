# Site Goal

## What I'm Trying to Accomplish

I want to build a Python-based system that runs a lead-generation and web design sales pipeline almost entirely on its own. The purpose of this system is to find local businesses that need a website, show them what a better website could look like, pitch it to them, and convert interested ones into paying clients that I personally build for.

## The End State I Want

A mostly autonomous agent that handles the front of my sales funnel — discovery, sample creation, and outreach — while keeping me in control of the moments that actually matter: talking to interested customers and deciding when to build the real thing.

## What the System Does

1. **Finds targets.** It searches maps for local businesses and identifies the ones that either have no website at all or have an outdated, low-quality website. These businesses are my prospects.

2. **Builds a sample.** For each qualifying business, it automatically generates a sample website — a tailored mockup showing what their improved web presence could look like.

3. **Cold emails them.** It reaches out to each business with a cold email that includes or links to the sample website. The goal of the email is to get them to see the sample, like it, and want me to build the real, full version that they pay me for.

4. **Notifies me on interest.** When a business replies and expresses interest in getting more, the system notifies me. At that point I take over the conversation and deal with the customer directly.

5. **Waits for my go-ahead, then builds the full site.** After I've handled the interested customer, the agent asks me whether it should begin building the full website. Only when I tell it to proceed does it build the complete, production website.

6. **The customer pays me.** The outcome of the whole pipeline is a paying client whose finished website I deliver.

## The Division of Labor I Want

- **The agent runs autonomously for:** finding targets, qualifying them, building samples, sending cold emails, and watching for replies.
- **I stay in the loop for:** being notified when someone is interested, personally communicating with that customer, and giving the explicit command that triggers the agent to build the full website.

## The Core Outcome

A repeatable, hands-off prospecting and pitching machine that fills my pipeline with warm, interested local-business leads who want me to build their website — so that my involvement is focused on closing the deal and authorizing the final build, and the result is customers paying me for completed websites.
